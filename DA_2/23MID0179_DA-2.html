Creamy pasta

Calories: 520 Protein: 15g Carbs: 68g Fat: 20g

Ingredients

  • 200g Pasta: Acts as the main base of the dish and provides energy.
  • 1 Cup Cream: Creates a rich, smooth, and creamy sauce.
  • 2 Cloves Garlic: Adds aroma and enhances flavor.
  • Salt and Pepper: Used to balance and enhance taste.

Instructions

  1. Boil pasta in salted water until soft but slightly firm (8-10 minutes).
  2. Heat oil in a pan and cook chopped garlic until it smells good.
  3. Add cream to the pan and add salt and pepper.
  4. Drain pasta and mix it with the creamy sauce for 2-3 minutes.
  5. Add your favorite toppings (parsley, cheese) and serve hot.

Chicken Stir-Fry

Calories: 380 Protein: 32g Carbs: 25g Fat: 18g

Ingredients

  • 300g Chicken Breast: Lean protein source.
  • Mixed Vegetables: Add color, nutrients, and crunch.
  • Soy Sauce: Provides salty and umami flavor.
  • Sesame Oil: Adds strong aroma.
  • Ginger: Enhances taste and aids digestion.

Instructions

  1. Cut chicken breast into small pieces.
  2. Heat sesame oil in a large pan over high heat.
  3. Cook chicken pieces while stirring until golden brown (5-6 minutes).
  4. Add mixed vegetables and cook while stirring for 3-4 minutes.
  5. Add soy sauce and chopped ginger, mix well and serve hot.

Margherita Pizza

Calories: 650 Protein: 24g Carbs: 75g Fat: 28g

Ingredients

  • Pizza Dough: Forms the base.
  • Tomato Sauce: Adds moisture and tanginess.
  • Mozzarella Cheese: Melts smoothly.
  • Fresh Basil: Adds aroma and freshness.
  • Olive Oil: Improves texture and flavor.

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 250°C (480°F).
  2. Flatten pizza dough on a baking sheet.
  3. Spread tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  4. Top with mozzarella cheese and pour olive oil on top.
  5. Bake for 12-15 minutes until edges are crispy. Add fresh basil and serve.

Caesar Salad

Calories: 320 Protein: 12g Carbs: 18g Fat: 22g

Ingredients

  • Romaine Lettuce: Crunchy base ingredient.
  • Caesar Dressing: Creamy flavor component.
  • Parmesan Cheese: Adds saltiness.
  • Croutons: Provide crunch.
  • Lemon Juice: Balances richness.

Instructions

  1. Wash lettuce well and cut into small pieces.
  2. Place cut lettuce in a large bowl.
  3. Add Caesar dressing and mix until lettuce is fully covered.
  4. Top with shredded parmesan cheese and croutons.
  5. Pour lemon juice over the salad and serve right away.

Chocolate Brownies

Calories: 450 Protein: 6g Carbs: 52g Fat: 26g

Ingredients

  • Dark Chocolate: Primary flavor ingredient.
  • Butter: Adds moisture and richness.
  • Eggs: Bind ingredients together.
  • Sugar: Sweetens the brownies.
  • Flour: Provides structure.

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 180°C and put oil/butter in a baking pan.
  2. Melt chocolate and butter together in a bowl.
  3. Mix eggs and sugar until light and airy.
  4. Add chocolate mixture and gently mix in flour.
  5. Bake for 25-30 minutes. Let it cool before cutting.

Vegetable Curry

Calories: 280 Protein: 8g Carbs: 32g Fat: 14g

Ingredients

  • 3 cups mixed vegetables
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced

Instructions

  1. Cook chopped onion and garlic in oil until soft.
  2. Add curry powder and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Add vegetables and mix well.
  4. Pour in coconut milk and cook slowly for 15 minutes.
  5. Serve hot with rice or naan bread.
